Hold the Keep: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Hold the Keep does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • The game works fully offline.

Game Center (optional). If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your best wave on each map to Game Center so it can appear on the leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive or store your Game Center details. You can turn Game Center off at any time in your device's Settings.

Your progress (crowns, upgrades, best waves and settings) is saved only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes this data. We never see it.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
